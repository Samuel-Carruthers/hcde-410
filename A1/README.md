# A1: Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St (hourly bike and pedestrian counts, by direction)
**Source:** https://cos-data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Burke-Gilman-Trail-north-of-NE-70th-St-Bicycle-Cou/2z5v-ecg8 (pulled 2026-10-13)
**Publisher:** City of Seattle, Seattle Department of Transportation
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain, as listed on the dataset's page. No attribution is required, but I credit the City of Seattle as the source.

## Questions I asked
1. When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest?
2. Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day?
3. Is there evidence that people use the trail to commute, not just for fun?
4. Are mornings mostly one direction and evenings the other, and what does that say about who uses the trail?

## How to re-run
Open `A1.ipynb` and run all cells (needs `requests`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`).
